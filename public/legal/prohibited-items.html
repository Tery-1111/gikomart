Prohibited Items

Effective [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

1. What you may not sell or post

GikoMart is a campus marketplace for lawful goods and services. The categories below are prohibited. This list follows the Prohibited Content & Conduct section of our Terms of Service and the kinds of content our automatic content filter screens for. It is not exhaustive: if a listing is unlawful, harmful, or misleading, it does not belong on GikoMart even if it is not named here.

  • Illegal goods or services of any kind, including stolen property.
  • Fake or replica branded goods, and any goods presented as genuine that are not.
  • Controlled substances and illegal drugs.
  • Weapons and items designed or adapted to cause harm.
  • Explosives and hazardous materials.
  • Adult or sexually explicit material and services.
  • Gambling, betting, and other games of chance for money.
  • Human organs, tissue, or bodily products offered for sale.
  • Age-restricted items offered without a lawful basis.
  • Content that infringes anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy, or other rights.
  • Harassment, abuse, threats, or impersonation of any person.
  • False, misleading, fraudulent, or deceptive listings.
  • Listings that ask a buyer to pay in advance outside the platform, or to hand over payment credentials.
  • Scraping, spam, or automated access to the Service without our written permission.
  • Anything designed to avoid a fee, a security feature, or a moderation control.

2. What happens to listings that break these rules

  • New listings and stores are screened automatically when they are posted. A listing that matches the automatic filter is hidden from public view and is not broadcast to the WhatsApp groups.
  • Edits are screened again, so an item that was published cleanly and later edited to break these rules is hidden too. A clean edit never clears a flag that an administrator has set.
  • Administrators can flag or remove a listing, and can flag, remove or suspend a store.
  • A phone number that has been blocked cannot start a payment.
  • Removing a listing is permanent, and a removed listing can no longer be edited or attached to a store.

GikoMart uses an automatic filter as a first pass; it does not check every listing by a person, and we do not promise a response time. Reports and administrator action decide what happens next.

Fees for a listing, boost or store plan are governed by the Fees & Payments section of our Terms of Service.

3. How to report a listing or store

Use the Report button on any listing or store page and choose a reason. You can also send the listing title and a short reason to [SUPPORT_WHATSAPP] or [SUPPORT_EMAIL].
